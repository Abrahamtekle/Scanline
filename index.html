Process

Three steps, all on your machine

No queue, no server round-trip — the recognition engine loads once and runs locally for every page you drop in.

01

Drop your pages

Add one scan or a whole stack. Reorder isn't needed — just drop them in the order you want them read.

02

Watch it read

A local OCR engine sweeps each page, picking out words, their positions, and their colors as it goes.

03

Export & edit

Fix anything it missed, then export a look-alike editable PDF, a plain-text PDF, or a plain .txt file.

Guaranteed pixel-perfect

Exact-match PDF

The original image, completely untouched — nothing is painted over or redrawn, so it looks 100% identical to your scan. A precisely-positioned invisible text layer sits on top so you can select, copy, and search it. Not retypeable, since nothing was replaced.

For screenshots & forms

Editable PDF

Paints over each recognized word and redraws it as real, live text in an approximate matching color. You can click in and retype words in a real PDF editor — but because pixels are replaced, it won't match the original as exactly as the option above.

For reflowable documents

Plain-text PDF

Drops the image and rebuilds your edited text above as a clean, reflowable document — no colors or layout, just the words.
