Editable PDF

Paints over each recognized word and redraws it as real, live text in an approximate matching color — everything else (panels, borders, shading) stays the original image. Open it in a real PDF editor (Acrobat, Preview, an online PDF editor) to click into a word and retype it. Most basic viewers like Chrome or Adobe Reader only let you select/copy text, not retype it — that's a viewer limitation, not this PDF's.
