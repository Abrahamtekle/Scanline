How this works: each page is rasterized in-browser with pdf.js, read by a local Tesseract OCR engine, then reassembled into a new PDF with pdf-lib — the recognized words are drawn back onto the page at zero opacity so viewers can select/search/copy text that lines up with what's printed underneath. Recognition quality depends on scan resolution and clarity, same as any OCR.
